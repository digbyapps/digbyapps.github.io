Privacy

Last updated 16 November 2026

The short version

Digby apps have no accounts and no analytics. Your videos stay on your Mac. Nothing is sold, shared or tracked.

Digby Video Compressor

  • Everything happens on your Mac. Your videos are never uploaded.
  • There’s no account and no analytics.
  • The encoders it uses (FFmpeg with SVT-AV1, x265, x264 and Opus) are built into the app, so it doesn’t download anything to work.

Digby Video Downloader

  • To download a video, it connects to the site in the link you paste, the same way your browser would.
  • It installs and updates its download engines (yt-dlp, FFmpeg and Deno) by itself, which means connecting to GitHub (for yt-dlp and Deno) and to ffmpeg.martin-riedl.de (for FFmpeg). Each engine is checked against its publisher’s checksum before it’s installed.
  • Links, files and sign-ins stay on your Mac. There’s no account and no analytics.
  • If YouTube asks you to sign in, Digby can use your browser’s YouTube sign-in. It reads it fresh each time and never stores it.
  • Diagnostic reports are never sent automatically. “Copy Report” puts one on your clipboard, with links, cookies and your user name removed, so you decide whether to share it and with whom.

This website

  • No cookies, no analytics and no tracking.
  • Fonts load from Google Fonts, so Google sees your IP address when you visit.
  • Downloads come from GitHub, which keeps its own logs.
  • If you use the light/dark switch, your browser remembers the choice locally. It isn’t sent anywhere.
